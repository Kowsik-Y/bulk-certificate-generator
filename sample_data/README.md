### How to Use

#### Option A: Quick-Fill in the Web UI
Open the "+ Generate New Batch" modal in the web app and click **"Load Sample 1"** or **"Load Sample 2"** to automatically populate all fields, recipients, and signature with 1 click.

#### Option B: Manual Upload
1. In the modal, click **"Upload JSON / Excel / CSV"** and choose `sample_recipients_1.csv` or `sample_recipients_2.csv`.
2. Under "Authorized Signature Image", click **"Upload Signature"** and choose `sample_signature_1.png` or `sample_signature_2.png`.
3. Select your desired template (Classic Gold or Modern Slate & Sky).
4. Click **"Generate Certificates"**.
